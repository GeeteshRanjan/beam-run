## 2b. MODEL CHANGE — read before touching gameplay (supersedes doc 01 §2/§6/§7)

The owner approved a redesign of the *meaning layer* after launch. The six-screen
structure, art direction, physics and budgets are unchanged; the progression model
is not. Where doc 01 disagrees with this section, **this section wins** (the docs
predate the change; `analytics-events.json` has been updated to match).

1. **One currency: months.** No lives, no score, no `DEATH`/`GAMEOVER` states.
   Clearing a screen books its `monthsBase`; the six bases sum to
   `JOURNEY.ANSR_BENCHMARK_MONTHS` (11) so a clean run lands exactly on ANSR's
   published benchmark. A hazard books `SETBACK_MONTHS` (2) and drops the player
   back on the last solid ground — a *setback*, handled inside `PLAYING`. The
   total is capped at `MAX_MONTHS` (23) so a run always beats the going-alone
   baseline (24) and the story can never invert.
2. **Every screen is a before/after of one problem.** Hazard instances sit on
   BOTH sides of a mid-screen badge: struggle zone (the buyer's current reality)
   → ANSR badge → relief zone (same hazard, yielding). The CI validator fails the
   build if either side is empty. We never label or offer a "do it yourself"
   route — that would legitimise self-build, which is the actual competitor.
3. **Four distinct verbs, not one reskinned shield.** `PLACE_TILE` builds a
   permanent bridge (1Wrk) · `EXTINGUISH` puts hiring lanes out for good as you
   approach (Talent500) · `CLEAR_PATH` lifts approval barriers for good (GCC-BOT)
   · `FORESIGHT` shows landing spots and stops setbacks without pausing the market
   (500Leaders). Help **never expires** — a 5-second shield would say "ANSR helps
   you briefly then leaves".
4. **Setback copy blames the system, never the player**, and setback popups are
   never orange (orange stays the value accent).
5. **The receipt is the conversion surface.** The win screen shows the run's
   months plus two *attributed* reference lines (ANSR average, going-alone
   average), and the four capability rows are themselves Navigator links carrying
   a declared `br_topic`. Leaving mid-run shows the same receipt (`summary`
   overlay) so nobody exits empty-handed. Intent is declared, never inferred from
   setback counts.
6. **One-tap auto-run is the default on touch.** The audience is executives on
   phones, not gamers.
